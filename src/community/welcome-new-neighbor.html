Welcome a New Neighbor

I had an opportunity to welcome a new neighbor to the neighborhood. She is a single mother from Indonesia with two children, a boy 17 and a girl 14. When I approached her she was busy moving furniture from the driveway into the house. I offered my help and I handed her one of the CFC welcome bags.

I could see the smile in her eyes and on her face as she accepted the welcome bag. I told her who I was and I wrote down all of my information on the tag attached to the bag. It really was a joy for me to be able to welcome her to the neighborhood by way of a gift from CFC.

The whole experience moved me out from my comfort zone into a place of caring and love through Jesus.

— Stephen S., Ashburn
