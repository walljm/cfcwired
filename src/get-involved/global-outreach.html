The Road Less Traveled

It is the heartbeat of God to redeem the world. Jesus commands his followers to go everywhere and tell everyone the Good News. We know that it’s not always possible for everyone to travel halfway around the world to provide help where help is needed.

There are those who are in the right season of life, who seek adventure, who love to meet people from around the world, who want to use their unique gifts, those who take the road less traveled, and it’s you!

There are all kinds of ways to be involved: adopting a missionary, going on a mission trip, praying for regions of the world, donation support, and more. We strategically focus our efforts by coming alongside indigenous leaders and serving through community transformation, clean water filters, teaching English, sports outreach, prayer, co-laboring, and sharing the Good News of Jesus.
