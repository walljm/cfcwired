LOCAL OUTREACH

Love Where You Live

Christian Fellowship Church desires to be indispensably integrated in the community and to witness the reality of Jesus’ mission in the world. Local outreach helps CFC people serve neighbors, schools, families, and community partners around Ashburn and Northern Virginia.

Whether you are serving at an event, meeting a practical need, mentoring, praying, or simply showing up with consistent care, local outreach is one way we practice being a Spirit-directed church for the good of our community.
