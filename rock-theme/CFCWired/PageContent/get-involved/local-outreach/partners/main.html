Jail Ministry

To serve the needs of incarcerated men and women and their families, by bringing them hope through the Gospel of Jesus Christ, as well as their material and emotional needs. As God leads you, you can be involved by being a regular prayer warrior, Bible correspondence course grader, or a Bible study leader or mentor. Training, coaching, and encouragement are provided for all involved.

OneHeartDC

A movement uniting and mobilizing churches, church networks and other organizations to serve the metro Washington region and share the good news of the gospel. We want each of our 5 million neighbors to experience the love and message of Jesus Christ through one or more of our partners. For this to happen, every Christ-follower needs to go on mission together. OneHeartDC is creating an environment for collaboration that makes an impact where we live!

Mosaic

Our mission is to educate and empower those facing pregnancy decisions. We do this by providing compassionate and professional medical care. We provide a range of free, confidential pregnancy services and are here for you no matter what you decide is best for you and your future. We are a non-profit medical organization serving women facing unplanned pregnancies. Because we have community support, all services are at no cost.

Bridgefeeds

We exist to serve and minister to the physical and spiritual needs of those in Loudoun County. While food assistance is core to what we do, we also provide education, prayer support and assist with referrals for housing, legal and healthcare services. Our team of volunteers strive to make our food distribution a positive experience that promotes love, dignity and hope for everyone.
