Why Serve?

People who choose to serve those in need on their own grow more than those who only serve when directed by the Church. Our goal is to lead believers to be Christ-centered individuals who ponder how to serve others all the time as led by the Spirit. When we serve, we add value to those in need. When you are sensitive to the Spirit, every place you step can be a moment God can use.

There is no place Jesus will not go in our city. There is no person Jesus will not meet with. If we are going to help people the way God wants, we must act as Jesus would.

When we respond differently than the world expects, with grace and mercy, instead of with judgment and condemnation, people take notice of Jesus in us.

We look forward to working with you as you bring your gifts, talents, resources, and God-given skills to advance the Gospel. (Proverbs 31:8, James 1:27, Matthew 25)
