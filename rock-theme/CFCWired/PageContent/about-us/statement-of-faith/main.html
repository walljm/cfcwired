ESSENTIAL FOR SALVATION

We believe Jesus is the only Son of God, who came to earth as a man, died for the sins of the world, and rose again victorious over sin, death and the grave. We believe Jesus' victory makes it possible that anyone who believes in him can be put back into right relationship with God the Father which guarantees life in eternity to come and facilitates life to the full in the present. All who put their faith in Jesus as their Savior become part of God's family.

We welcome all who hold this belief as our brothers and sisters in Christ and invite them to call Christian Fellowship Church their church home.
